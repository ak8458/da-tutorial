PUBLIC SAFETY’S NETWORK

FirstNet Promise

FirstNet delivers a second-to-none experience for public safety, helping to ensure first responders have priority access and uninterrupted connectivity during emergencies and is the only network mandated to never throttle public safety traffic.

What is the FirstNet Promise

Your trusted partner in community safety

Coverage everywhere public safety goes

FirstNet gives first responders First Priority®, speed, reliability and the 5G connectivity they need to communicate.

Accountable to public safety, always

FirstNet is expanding based on public safety’s direct feedback with a focus in rural, remote and tribal areas across the country.​

Reliable, secure connectivity

With the first and only dedicated Security Operations Center, FirstNet goes well beyond standard commercial network security measures.

Network investments and visibility

Delivering a second-to-none experience for public safety

Investing in safety

FirstNet is the largest network for public safety and the only network with enough capacity to support every first responder in the country.

First responders’ jobs demand more than commercial networks can provide. It's why the federal government and AT&T continuously invest and innovate based on public safety’s needs. This helps ensure they have mission-tested and mission-ready technology for critical moments – helping them save lives and protect their communities.

Unprecedented network visibility

First responders asked for transparency of their network and a secure portal to enhance safety and efficiency during emergencies.

FirstNet Central was built to allow agencies to access network conditions in near real-time, adjust user priority levels and allow multisystem interoperability.

FirstNet gives you the best pricing on plans and trade-in offers so you can have the latest device – wherever the mission takes you.

Customer care

Dedicated connectivity, dedicated care

FirstNet Care

For public safety, seconds can save lives. That’s why FirstNet has dedicated care to assist in public safety's unique needs to meet the urgency you require. Their specialization in public safety helps them quickly address requests to keep your communications up and running.

First responders come first on the only network built with and for public safety.

FirstNet technology and solutions

Innovations driven by public safety needs that enhance community safety

Dedicated connectivity

Band 14 is spectrum set aside specifically for FirstNet, so public safety never competes with commercial traffic. First responders have always-on priority and preemption, wherever the next mission takes them.

Learn more
Mission-critical solutions

The first to deliver nationwide mission-critical push-to-talk, FirstNet enhances performance and situational awareness to further interoperability and public safety innovation.

Learn more
Disaster response operations

The Response Operations Group™  (ROG) is comprised of dedicated, former first responders who provide public safety around-the-clock support. Directly linked to State Emergency Operations Centers, ROG ensures first responders stay connected. 

Learn more
Strongest signal devices

FirstNet pioneered connectivity for public safety in hard-to-reach environments, and is the only network that gives first responders access to FirstNet MegaRange™. These devices deliver the strongest signal available in the U.S.¹

Learn more

¹This enhanced signal is available only on Band 14 spectrum, the spectrum to which AT&T has exclusive access for FirstNet.
